Operators can compare pinned slskR and slskd connected-idle CPU and memory readings from `kspld0`. The report separates DHT-off/on profiles, records an aborted `kspls0` attempt under severe saturation, and links raw trials, session checks, and host-load context. Results do not predict other hosts or active workloads.
