## Arabic compatibility-history day shorthand

In `CompatibilityDashboard.jsx`, labels `7d`, `30d`, `90d`, and `180d` all select day ranges (7, 30, 90, and 180 days). The Arabic catalog uses `7 د`, `30 د`, `90 د`, and `180 د` for those same options. This same-component comparison supports the intended compact day meaning of the Arabic `د` in this selected label.
